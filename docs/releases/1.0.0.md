# 玄象 1.0.0

玄象 1.0 is a structured knowledge, classical-text, relationship, and deterministic-study platform. It includes public knowledge browsing/search, classical-text reading and citations, graph exploration, Bazi Engine 1.2.0 with STANDARD_ZIPING 1.1.0, Liuyao Engine 1.0.0 with STANDARD_WENWANG_GUA 1.0.0, and an authenticated editorial workflow when its production controls are accepted.

The optional AI/RAG layer explains published knowledge, cited passages, and server-verified calculation traces. It does not calculate Bazi/Liuyao results, read drafts, edit source text, publish directly, predict fate, or divine outcomes. AI may be disabled independently of the Core release.

## Known limitations

- Liuyao remains experimental/ready with documented limitations; it is not a claim of authoritative completeness.
- Lunar input remains beta.
- AI availability and quality depend on separately accepted chat/embedding providers and a compatible production index.
- Content is curated research material with provenance/status labels, not professional, medical, legal, financial, or life-decision advice.
- Community, comments, messages, payments, subscriptions, mobile apps, mini-programs, a third divination system, strength/pattern/useful-god/response-time analysis, AI fate reading, and AI divination are not part of 1.0.

As of 2026-09-20 the repository contains readiness mechanisms and documentation, but production database, staging, backup/restore, provider, remote CI, dependency-audit closure, and smoke evidence are absent. Public launch remains **BLOCKED** until the launch checklist is reviewed and satisfied.
