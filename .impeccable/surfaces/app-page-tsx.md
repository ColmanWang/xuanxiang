## Scope and mode

- Scope: global brand shell, homepage, graph entry and research-mode disclosure.
- Visitor mode: Read, with an Operate transition inside graph and deduction surfaces.

## Audience, job and action

- Audience: traditional-culture learners and researchers who want sourced relationships, not prediction claims.
- Job: understand an idea quickly, then trace it through eras, classics, people, schools and techniques.
- Primary actions: enter the knowledge base; explore the Xuanxiang graph.

## Chosen direction

- World: digital archive conservation desk / museum accession system, rendered as Chinese classical futurism.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/home-option-c-approved.png`.
- Memorable moment: a coordinate-based relation field links 周易、阴阳、五行、天干地支、八字与奇门 in the first viewport; later it expands into the dark “象之网络” field.

## Global grammar

- Warm mineral-white background, carbon ink, patinated deep teal, desaturated jade, restrained cinnabar marker.
- Hairline geometry, square/small radii, no glass, glow, gold, gradients or ornamental bagua.
- Chinese serif display, system sans UI, tabular numerals for years and coordinates.
- Motion: one slow relation-line reveal/focus transition, disabled under reduced motion.

## Constraints

- No fortune-telling copy or fabricated scholarly proof.
- Core UI and graph labels remain code-native.
- Research mode reveals provenance without duplicating pages.
- Responsive layouts must preserve tables via deliberate scrolling or semantic stacking.
